OUR HERITAGE

A Business Built Across Markets

Aristocrat Fine Foods grew from a friendship formed in Hong Kong between Steven H. Vinik and Cristian Giancaterino, and from a shared belief that the quality of a meal begins long before it reaches the table. Steven brought a career in international finance and a perspective shaped by global business. Cris brought culinary experience, product knowledge and relationships developed through fine food and hospitality.

A Shared Standard

Across Asia, Europe and the United Kingdom, they saw how exceptional ingredients reached hotels, casinos, restaurants and private tables through judgment, trust and long-standing relationships. The best products were not always the most public. What mattered was whether the ingredient, source and relationship could withstand close attention.

From Hospitality to the American Table

Aristocrat brought that curatorial standard to local customers, not simply to reproduce a European idea of fine food, but to make remarkable ingredients more accessible and approachable within American life. As new relationships and opportunities developed, the business expanded into wholesale, distribution, Chef to Chef, foodservice, hospitality, importing and international export.

Independent by Principle

Aristocrat's integrity depends on remaining larger than any single producer or supplier. Independence allows the company to respond when quality, availability, pricing or market conditions change and to recommend what is right for the client rather than what is most convenient for the vendor. It also supports attention to authenticity, origin, environmental impact and the long-term health of the relationships behind the food.

Norfolk, Virginia and the Chesapeake

Aristocrat Fine Foods is based in Norfolk, Virginia, within the Chesapeake region. Water, seafood, maritime commerce and the movement of goods have shaped this place for generations. For a company working between American consumers, international producers and global food markets, Norfolk is a natural point of connection between source, trade and table.

Today, Aristocrat is a fine-food house, a sourcing network and a commercial advisor. Products and sources will evolve; the standard, the independence and the belief in beginning with the ingredient will remain.
